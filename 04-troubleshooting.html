Common Issues Across All Models

Issue No water flow from group head

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Empty or unlocked water tank → Fill and fully lock the tank

Issue No hot water / no steam

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Tank empty, not at temperature, or descale overdue → Fill, wait to heat, or run descale cycle

Issue Espresso drips or won't flow

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Grind too fine, overdosed, over-tamped, or blocked basket → Coarser grind, lower dose, correct tamp (10–15kg most models, 15–20kg Dual Boiler), unblock basket

Issue Espresso flows too fast

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Grind too coarse or underdosed → Finer grind, increase dose

Issue No crema

Possible cause(s): Stale beans, wrong basket type, or light tamp

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Fresh beans (5–20 days post "Roasted On")
  2. Dual Wall basket for pre-ground
  3. Tamp firmly

Issue Steam wand blocked / weak froth

Possible cause(s): Milk residue or scale buildup in tip

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Pin tool
  2. Unscrew and soak in hot water
  3. Descale if persistent

Issue Machine on but stops working

Possible cause(s): Safety thermal cut-out (overheat protection)

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Power off, unplug, cool 30–60+ minutes, retry
  2. Contact Sage support if it recurs

Issue Portafilter leaks around the edge

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Not locked in fully, grounds on rim, or worn silicone seal → Insert fully and rotate to resistance, clean rim, replace seal if worn

Issue No ground coffee from grinder

Possible cause(s): Empty hopper, blocked chute/burrs, or moisture

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Refill beans
  2. Clean grinder chamber and chute
  3. Dry thoroughly before reassembly

Issue Pump makes pulsing/loud noise

Possible cause(s): Normal pump operation, OR empty/unlocked tank, OR demineralised/distilled water

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Usually normal
  2. Otherwise fill/lock tank or switch to filtered tap water

Issue Descale/clean indicator on

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Scheduled maintenance due (every 60–200 shots or 60–90 days, model-dependent) → Run the model's cleaning or descale cycle
General care rules - all models Never immerse the cord, plug, or appliance body in water · hand-wash removable parts only, nothing is dishwasher safe · never use abrasive cleaners, pads, or alkaline agents · avoid demineralised, distilled, or heavily-filtered (RO) water - it degrades taste and can damage the machine · replace the water filter every 2–3 months (model-dependent) · use freshly roasted whole beans, never grind unroasted/under-roasted beans · if a fault won't clear after the recommended cool-down/retry, escalate to Sage/Breville Customer Service - attempting an internal repair voids the warranty.

Known Issue Deep-Dives

Policy - Oracle Jet (SES985) tamper fan Do NOT advise customers to change tamper fan settings. The factory ships the correct settings (2 lines); manipulating this on a new unit has repeatedly caused inconsistent brewing. Only amend once the machine is old and the beans are no longer very fresh.
  • Customer wants less coffee ground / wavy tamping → advise lowering the grind size setting instead.
  • Coffee doesn't run through → advise a coarser grind instead.
Known escalation pattern: Oracle Jet consistently doses 21–23g despite correct grind, max tamp height, and a standard double basket → treat as a possible factory-defect part (tamper-fan connector and/or double filter basket), not user error. See the worked example on the Sample Call page.
Latch stuck - SES985 Oracle-family Can get stuck due to scale buildup. Fix: mix descaler with a little water into a paste, apply to the edge of the latch, leave a few minutes. Do NOT force the latch open - risk of damage. If it's still stuck after descaler treatment, proceed with a repair.
Known issue - Dual Boiler (SES920) turns on by itself Random times, often flagged via the app. Work through in order:
  1. Note the current software version, the time it powered on, and whether other appliances were in use.
  2. Power-cycle: unplug from mains ~20 seconds, check if resolved.
  3. If not on the latest software, recommend updating.
  4. If it still recurs after update: check for a newer software update, then have the customer do a factory reset.
  5. If still unresolved: check again for a software update; if none, apologise and offer a replacement.
Known issue - Dual Boiler inconsistent shot with auto dial-in
  1. Check the beans - dark roast works better with Dual Boiler; use the "roasted on" date, not "best before."
  2. Try switching between volume-based and time-based auto dial-in.
  3. After changing grind size, do a double-tap purge of the grinder so the next dose is entirely the new grind size.
  4. Check/adjust the tamper fan height/connector (setup guidance was under product-team review at time of writing).

Troubleshooting steps

Issue FROTH and TEMPERATURE lights flash together

Troubleshooting steps
  1. Steam function error → Press and hold STEAM 3 sec to purge system
  2. Water tank may be empty → fill tank

Alert lights: lower steam wand alert (MILK TEMP/FOAM flash downward if wand left raised - lower wand, hold STEAM 5s to purge) · blocked mid-texturing (machine stops, STEAM light off, MILK TEMP/FOAM fully lit and flashing until manual purge) · cleaning due after 200 extractions (1 CUP/2 CUP alternate flash) · descale due (1 CUP+STEAM / 2 CUP alternate flash 15 sec) · low water (buttons unlit).

Maintenance: cleaning cycle - disc+tablet, hold 1 CUP+2 CUP 5 sec · descaling every 90 days (hardness 4) / 60 days (hardness 6), remove filter first, hold 1 CUP+STEAM 5 sec · steam wand wipe after every use · factory reset - off (hold 1 CUP+STEAM 5s) → hold 1 CUP+2 CUP 5s → press 1 CUP or 2 CUP (3 flashes = confirmed).

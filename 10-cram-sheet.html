Golden Rules - Never / Always

Never advise Oracle Jet tamper fan changes

Advise grind size instead. See policy.

Never force a stuck SES985 latch

Use descaler paste, wait, retry - or escalate to repair.

Never say "nothing I can do" / "that's our policy" / "I don't know"

Use the ANCHOR-approved alternatives instead. See table.

Never toggle case comments Public

They stay private, always.

Never sell a machine at full price

Machines are Accommodation or Sale-at-0-cost only. Full/discounted price = spare parts and accessories only.

Never promise a repair/replacement/refund before approval

Goodwill is a judgment call, not an entitlement.

Never use demineralised/distilled/RO water in any machine

Damages the machine and hurts taste.

Never grind unroasted/under-roasted beans

Burr damage risk, explicitly a safety issue on some models.

Always fill in Vital Signs + case comment

Every route ends: email the customer, then case comment.

Always escalate to Tier 2 in two parts

Escalate tab, THEN comment on it again in Feed with the specification template.

Always open a separate case per item

Even for a full-order refund.

Always match the ANCHOR flow

Acknowledge (+ Honour) → Navigate → Commit → Own → Reassure. Resolve first contact where possible.

Gotchas & Flagged Contradictions

1 · Full-price sales route One source frames "full price" as an existing sales route for spare parts (§7 of the Salesforce process); a later cancellations note says a sale at full price can't be made at all because the customer orders from the site. Both agree machines are never full-price. Confirm with your trainer which wording applies to your team.
2 · Glossary items still unsettled "IT" possibly should be "ITA" (clash with Information Technology) · "SM" possibly should mean Service Manual, not ScreenMeet · "WH" is warehouse, not to be confused with Windhorn (no abbreviation assigned yet). See full glossary.
3 · Dimensions/weight are never in the manuals Don't guess or fabricate a number for any model's dimensions or weight - they're rating-label only, not printed as manual text.

Self-Check Questions

Click to reveal each answer. Try answering out loud first.

What are the 4 Keys and their target values?

Dose 18–22g · Pressure 9 bar · Temperature 93°C · Steam 130°C. See Espresso Fundamentals.

A shot pours fast, tastes sour, and has pale crema. What's wrong and what's the fix?

Under-extracted - grind too coarse. Fix: grind finer. See Reading a Shot.

Which two machines don't have a three-way solenoid valve?

Bambino (SES500) and Duo Temp Pro (SES810). See Machine Components.

Which filter basket type is recommended for a customer with no grinder using pre-ground coffee?

Dual Wall / pressurized basket. See Filter Baskets.

Which two models use The Impress™ Puck System, and which one uses Auto MilQ™?

Impress Puck System: SES876 and SES882. Auto MilQ™: SES985 (Oracle Jet) only. See Feature Legend.

A customer's Oracle Jet keeps dosing 21–23g. What do you NOT tell them to do, and what's the likely real cause?

Do not tell them to adjust the tamper fan setting. If grind, tamp height, and basket are all correct, treat this as a likely factory defect (tamper-fan connector or double basket) and escalate - not user error. See Tamper Fan policy and the Sample Call.

Dual Boiler (SES920) keeps turning itself on randomly. What's the 5-step process?

1) Note SW version, power-on time, other appliances in use. 2) Power-cycle (unplug ~20s). 3) Update software if not latest. 4) If it recurs, check for a newer update, then factory reset. 5) If still unresolved, check again for an update; if none, apologise and offer replacement. See Known Issue.

What are the six letters of ANCHOR and what does each mean?

Acknowledge, Navigate, Commit, Honour, Own, Reassure. See ANCHOR Model.

A customer demands a manager. What's the ANCHOR-approved response?

"Of course, I can help with that. Before I arrange the next step, let me capture the full picture so the right person can review it properly." See Handling Angry Customers.

What's the difference between "goodwill" and an entitlement?

Goodwill protects customer trust in justified situations - it is never a public promise or a standard entitlement, and it's a judgment call, not automatic. See Goodwill Policy.

Out-of-warranty coffee grinder - what does Sage offer?

No repair offered. Offer 30% discount on a new appliance (Tier 2 for more). See Warranty by Category.

A Nespresso-compatible Sage machine breaks 5 months after purchase. In warranty or not - who handles it?

Still in-warranty rules apply only for the first 3 months (replace). Past 3 months, in-warranty issues go to Nespresso directly. See Warranty by Category.

A customer bought an "RM" unit from idoodirect. Who handles the warranty claim?

The retailer (idoodirect/Windhorn), not Sage - RM units carry their warranty with the seller. See RM Units.

Order never arrived - was it a machine or a spare part? What route, and what's different about the machine case?

Both routes are "Sale at 0 cost." The machine version additionally requires the 103-DPD Declaration of Non-Receipt form and an escalation to ORDER before the sale is created - spare parts skip straight to creating the 0-cost sale. See The Six Order Routes.

What comes right after pressing NEW ORDER, on every route?

Select the shipping method. See Patterns Worth Memorising.

Which two things only appear on the machine Accommodation route, and nowhere else?

Return label and return location. See Patterns Worth Memorising.

Order Grand Total is 35 EUR and you're refunding it fully. What do you check before refunding, and why?

Check Financial Information for shipping costs before refunding - if shipping costs are included, use the manual refund process instead of the automatic Refund button, and explain why in your case comment. See The Shipping Cost Trap.

What are the two parts of a Tier 2 escalation, and what's the second part people forget?

First: escalate normally on the Escalate tab. Second (often forgotten): go to Feed, find your escalation, and comment on it with the specification template (Unit model specs, POP Y/N, Previous case Y/N, Current situation, Customer's expectations, Other info). See Escalation to Tier 2.

What does SIA stand for, and when does the deposit actually get charged?

Shipping In Advance. The deposit is only authorised, never charged upfront - it's charged only if the faulty item isn't delivered back to the warehouse within 20 days. See SIA.

What does "DIF" mean on the refund screen, and when does it apply?

Destroy In Field - the customer keeps the old/defective item, we don't need it back. Ticks itself automatically on spare parts refunds. See The Refund Flow Screen.

What does "RM" mean in the glossary, and is there a caveat?

Remanufactured. Caveat: this overlaps with the separate "RM units" third-party refurbished-machine topic (idoodirect/Windhorn) - same letters, related but distinct concepts. See Glossary.

You're working a shift on August 15. What's the pay impact and why?

+75% for that hour - August 15 (Assumption of the Virgin Mary) is an official Greek bank holiday under Β.Δ. 748/66. See Greek Bank Holidays.

What's the difference between "Available Phone & Case" and "Available Phone + Chat"?

"Available Phone & Case" is the default status for the whole shift (calls + assigned cases). "Available Phone + Chat" is calls-and-chats-only, and may only be used when a supervisor explicitly tells you to. See AUX / Status Codes.

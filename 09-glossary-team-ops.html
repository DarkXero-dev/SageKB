Greek Bank Holidays (Pay Rule)

Working a Sunday or an official Greek bank holiday increases that hour's wage by 75%, per Greek law (Β.Δ. 748/66).

  1. New Year's Day
  2. January 6 (Epiphany)
  3. March 25 (Independence Day)
  4. Easter Monday
  5. May 1 (Labour Day)
  6. August 15 (Assumption of the Virgin Mary)
  7. October 28 (National Anniversary)
  8. December 25 (Christmas Day)
  9. December 26 (Boxing Day)

AUX / Status Codes (Phone System)

Misuse - including excessive break time - gets reviewed and can affect bonus eligibility.

Available statuses

StatusUse
Available Phone & CaseYour default status for the whole shift, including overtime. Receives calls while working assigned cases. Cases are assigned automatically - handle what you receive, don't re-tag or reassign back to a colleague. Documentation is critical: always include reason for contact, questions asked and answers given, findings/discoveries, actions taken and advice given, timeframes and next steps communicated.
Available Phone + ChatCalls and chats only - use only when your supervisor explicitly tells you to
Available ChatWritten interactions, chats, and actionables - use only when explicitly advised by your supervisor
Do NOT use unless explicitly approved Available · Available Phone · Available Phone + Chat + Case · Available Chat + Case

Unavailable statuses

StatusRule
TrainingOnly for pre-approved sessions
MeetingOnly for pre-approved supervisor/ACM/team meetings
BreakShort break (2× 10 min): "On Break." Lunch break (25 min): "Lunch." Don't combine breaks or take breaks during the first/last hour of your shift, except emergencies (must be reported to your supervisor)
BusySupervisor-approved tasks only (quality monitoring, client assignments)
Extended ACWOnly to finalize a case immediately after a phone call. Never for actionables, breaks, or unrelated tasks

A Real-Time Management (RTM) person monitors AUX usage live during peak hours, in addition to supervisors. Unauthorized use of Training, Meeting, or extended breaks gets flagged and you'll be contacted immediately to correct it.

Goodwill Policy

Goodwill = Trust Protection, Not Entitlement Goodwill is not a public promise and should never be presented as a standard entitlement. It is a way to protect customer trust when the situation justifies further support - a judgment call, never something promised automatically.

When to consider it

Customer acting in good faith · standard answer feels unfair or brand-damaging · failure outside guarantee but not expected for a premium appliance · customer shows strong brand loyalty · repeated troubleshooting/delays/failed repairs/unclear communication · case has dragged on and customer is losing confidence.

What it can include

Replacement · extra troubleshooting · suitable alternative product (upgrades only with approval) · escalation for other goodwill gestures · repair contribution or adjusted discount · free repair with appropriate warranty extension.

What to Avoid in Customer Care

Treating the guarantee as the only measure of fairness · saying no too early without reviewing the full situation · implying goodwill is automatic · promising a repair/replacement/refund/upgrade/exception before approval · making the customer repeat their story unnecessarily · hiding behind internal process · sending the customer between teams without a clear handover · using technical language that doesn't help the customer · sounding surprised the customer expected the product to last · closing a case because the process is complete when the customer experience is still unresolved.

Unrepairable Machines

If the repair centre says a machine can't be repaired, first create a Repair Replacement order (same model as the original, not a different unit) in the original case: NEW ORDER → select shipping method → activate the order → email customer → case comment.

Full detail, including the missing-parts and packaging-damage variants, is below - this diagram is the fast path, not the whole policy.

Unrepairable - In Warranty

  • Purchased <36 months ago (more than 30), or scrapped prematurely by the service centre:
    • 900 series → replace with a new machine using L03
    • 800 series and smaller → offer 60% off any coffee machine
  • Repaired <12 months ago → replace with a new machine using L03

Unrepairable - Out of Warranty

  1. Purchased <48 months ago (more than 36), or brand-new but repaired <18 months ago → new unit at 50% off (escalate if customer wants a further discount)
  2. Missing spare parts:
    • Wait 4–6 weeks with an additional repair discount for the wait, or
    • New unit at 60% off if the customer doesn't want to wait, or
    • If both rejected: return the machine unrepaired at no cost, offer to notify the customer when repair becomes possible again
  3. Poor packaging / cosmetic damage: tell the customer our packaging instructions were clear, then ask the repair centre if a functional repair is possible with cosmetic damage left. If yes, share damage photos so the customer can decide.
    • 900 series → 50% off same series, or 40% off an 800/500/400 series
    • 800 series and smaller → 40% off same series, or 50% off a 900 series
  4. Europe only: if every offer is refused, ask Windhorn to offer an RM unit instead, at a cost we don't control and can't discount further. Email Windhorn from the case with the work order number. If in doubt, escalate to Tier 2/3.

Repeat Failure Cases

Log in the Service and QC Information section: tick "Repeat Repair Failure," fill in "Repeat Failure Reason," link "Original WO Number." "Days Until Repeat Failure" auto-calculates on save.

WindowRule
In Warranty (24+12 month grace) - unit <6 months oldReplace, if there's a clear defect (excluding maintenance issues)
In Warranty (24+12 month grace) - unit >6 months oldRepair. Max 1 repair, unless it's 100% clear the first repair/replacement was due to lack of maintenance - then the second fault can be repaired
Out of Warranty (beyond 24+12 months)Exceptions only via Tier 2 escalation - see specific rules below
  • SES900 series already repaired once within 24+12 months → repair FOC within 24 months after the grace period ends (within 5 years of original purchase)
  • SES900 series already repaired twice within 24+12 months → replace FOC within 24 months after grace period ends (within 5 years of original purchase)
  • Known recurring issues not caused by the customer (SES880 Error 5, SES990/SES980/SES920 under-heating, SES875 leaking from the brew head where the group head screws lacked Loctite, or similar) → repair FOC within 24 months after grace period (within 5 years of original purchase)
  • 1 repair within first 24+12 months → repair as out-of-warranty (OOW)
  • 2 repairs within 24+12 months → repair as in-warranty (IW), requesting a warranty extension

RM Units (Refurbished / Third-Party)

RM units are sold by idoodirect (UK, also on eBay) and Windhorn (Europe). Customers who buy them have their warranty with the seller, not with Sage.

How to spot one: serial number includes "RM" · purchased from Windhorn or idoodirect.

Warranty: usually 12 months per their proof of purchase; claims go to the retailer, not Sage.

StatusIf troubleshooting fails
In warranty (within 12 months)Advise the customer to contact their retailer for a warranty claim
Out of warranty (after 12 months)Offer: (1) contact our repair centre for a paid out-of-warranty repair, or (2) purchase a new device at a discount

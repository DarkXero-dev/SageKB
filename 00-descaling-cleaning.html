Every model needs a cleaning cycle and a descale cycle on its own schedule. This page covers why it matters, the difference between the two, the general care rules that apply to every machine, the exact steps for all 11 models as documented, and how to walk a customer through it - whether they're comfortable following technical instructions or need it broken down step by step.

Why It Matters

Straight from the source "Machine hygiene: adherence to cleaning/descaling cycle is a MUST, in addition to daily cleaning." The Perfect Single Shot Basics list is just as direct: "descaling/cleaning cadence is a MUST" alongside pressure, dose, and tamp.

Almost every "no steam," "no hot water," "espresso only drips or not at all," and "steam wand blocked" row across the Common Issues table and every per-model fault table lists needs descaling or needs cleaning as a possible cause. A customer who's fallen behind on both is the single most common reason a machine "suddenly" stops working right.

Cleaning vs. Descaling

Cleaning Cycle

Fits the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution into the basket, then runs a cycle through the group head and brew path. Grinder-equipped models also get a separate grinder cleaning step - documented on the Barista Touch (SES880) as clearing oil build-up, and on several other models as clearing blockages between the burrs and outlet.

Triggered by an extraction counter (200 shots on several models) or a CLEAN ME / Clean Cycle alert.

Descale Cycle

Clears scale (limescale) buildup that forms in the boiler, water lines, and steam wand from the water passing through the machine. Uses a descaling powder or sachet dissolved in water; the water filter is removed first on most models.

Triggered on a schedule tied to water hardness - the source material sets a faster cadence for harder water (e.g. every 60 days at hardness 6 vs. every 90 days at hardness 4 on the Bambino/Barista Express), or by a DESCALE alert.

General Care Rules (All Models)

Non-negotiables - every model, every time
  • Never immerse the cord, plug, or machine body in water.
  • Hand-wash removable parts only - nothing on any of these machines is dishwasher safe.
  • Never use abrasive cleaners, abrasive pads, or alkaline cleaning agents.
  • Avoid demineralised, distilled, or heavily-filtered (RO) water - use cold filtered tap water.
  • Replace the water filter every 2-3 months (exact interval is model-dependent - see the model card below).
  • Use freshly roasted whole beans; never grind unroasted or under-roasted beans.
  • If a fault won't clear after the recommended cool-down/retry, escalate to the relevant Sage/Breville Customer Service Centre - internal repair attempts void the warranty.

Step-by-Step, By Model

Button names, menu paths, and cadence differ by model - always confirm the model before giving steps. Steps below are transcribed directly from each model's own documentation, including where to put the descaling powder/solution and how much, wherever the source specifies it.

When the source doesn't give a quantity Some models (SES876, SES920, SES980) have a documented ratio - 1 sachet dissolved in 1L water. For every other model, the source material doesn't state an exact powder-to-water ratio - it's either on-screen guided (the display specifies the amount) or simply not given. Where that's the case, it's flagged below - use the dose printed on the descaling powder/solution packaging rather than guessing a number.
SES500 Bambino™ Plus

Cleaning cycle: insert the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution into the basket, then hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP together for 5 seconds. Triggered at 200 extractions (1 CUP/2 CUP lights alternate-flash). Exact quantity isn't specified for this model - use the dose printed on the packaging.

Descale cycle: remove the water filter from the tank first, dissolve descaling powder/solution in water, then hold 1 CUP + STEAM for 5 seconds. Cadence: every 90 days at water hardness 4, every 60 days at hardness 6 (descale-due alert: 1 CUP+STEAM / 2 CUP lights alternate-flash every 15s). This model uses a 2L descale container - the source doesn't detail the exact fill procedure beyond that capacity.

Steam wand: wipe and purge after every use. Water filter: replace every 3 months.

SES870 Barista Express™ (Safety Guide edition)

Same physical machine as SES875 below - use that card for the full cleaning/descale sequence. This document only confirms: the CLEAN ME light means the machine needs cleaning or descaling, and the water filter is replaced approximately every 90 days.

SES875 Barista Express™

Cleaning cycle: insert the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution into the basket, then hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP + POWER for about 3 seconds. The cycle runs about 5 minutes. Exact quantity isn't specified for this model - use the dose printed on the packaging.

Descale cycle: remove the water filter from the tank, dissolve descaling powder/solution in water and pour it into the tank, then hold 2 CUP + POWER. Runs 3 stages plus a rinse. Cadence: every 90 days at hardness 4, every 60 days at hardness 6 (or every 40L / 60L at hardness 2). Exact quantity isn't specified for this model - use the dose printed on the packaging.

Water filter: soak 5 minutes before fitting, then replace on the same 90-day/40L schedule. Steam wand: wipe and purge after every use. Grinder: if blocked between the burrs or outlet, advanced cleaning needs a 10mm socket. Group head seal: if leaking, replace the silicone seal with an Allen key.

SES876 Barista Express™ Impress

Cleaning cycle: fit the 1 CUP basket with the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution, then hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP + POWER for about 3 seconds. Runs about 5 minutes. The Maintenance Light flashes when a clean is due. Exact quantity isn't specified for this step - use the dose printed on the packaging.

Descale cycle: remove the water filter from the tank, dissolve 1 sachet of descaling powder/solution in 1L water and pour it into the tank, then hold 2 CUP + POWER for 5 seconds; repeat, then rinse. The Maintenance Light shows solid when a descale is due.

Water filter: replace every 3 months (soak 5 minutes, rinse 5 seconds). Grinder: conical burrs need brush cleaning; advanced disassembly if blocked. Tamp system: brush clean. Steam wand: wipe, purge, and unblock as needed. Group head seal: periodic silicone seal replacement.

SES878 Barista Pro™

FLUSH alert: fit the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution into the basket, empty the drip tray, fill the tank to MAX with water, then go Menu → FLUSH → 1 CUP. Exact powder quantity isn't specified for this step - use the dose printed on the packaging.

DESCALE alert: remove the water filter from the tank, dissolve descaling powder/solution in water and fill the tank to the descale line, then go Menu → DESCALE → 1 CUP. Runs 3 stages plus a rinse.

Grinder: conical burrs - unlock, empty, run empty, then brush; advanced disassembly for a blockage (burrs are sharp, handle with care). Steam wand: wipe, purge, and unblock as needed. Group head: wipe the shower screen, and periodically purge with an empty basket.

SES880 Barista Touch™

Cleaning cycle: on-screen guided, back-flush style - the display specifies where to add the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution and how much to use.

Descale cycle: Settings → Descale, fully on-screen guided (the display specifies the water tank fill amount). Never fully remove the water tank mid-cycle.

Water filter: every 90 days / 40L (60L at hardness 2). Grinder: has its own cleaning cycle, documented as clearing oil build-up. Steam wand: Settings-guided cleaning. Group head seal: 8-step guided replacement in Settings.

SES882 Barista Touch™ Impress

Cleaning cycle: on-screen guided, back-flush style - the display specifies where to add the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution and how much to use.

Descale cycle: Settings → Descale (the display specifies the water tank fill amount). Remove the water filter first. Never use bottled water, and never fully empty the tank mid-cycle.

Water filter: every 3 months (soak 5 minutes). Filter basket: pin + descaling powder/solution soak, about 20 minutes. Steam wand: Settings-guided cleaning, or manual pin/soak. Grinder/burrs: regular brushing plus advanced disassembly if needed. Tamp system: brush clean. Group head seal: multi-step guided replacement.

Beans Never use unroasted or under-roasted beans - risk of burr damage and a safety issue on this model specifically.
SES920 the Dual Boiler

Cleaning cycle: triggered by the CLEAN ME! icon, roughly every 200 shots. Fit the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution into the basket, run MANUAL; the cycle takes about 370 seconds. Exact quantity isn't specified for this step - use the dose printed on the packaging.

Descale cycle: takes about 1.5 hours. Machine must be off and cooled for at least 1 hour first. From off, hold 1 CUP then POWER for 3 seconds, select dESC via MENU, drain both boilers via their screws, dissolve 1 sachet of descaling powder/solution in 1L water and run it through the boilers, drain, then flush twice.

Water filter: every 2 months. Steam wand: wipe and purge every use.

Water Never use demineralised or distilled water in this machine.
SES980 the Oracle™

Cleaning cycle: fit the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution into the basket, then go MENU → CLEAN CYCLE. Also run a clear-water backflush after each session (disc fitted, 2 CUP, 20 seconds × 5). Exact quantity isn't specified for this step - use the dose printed on the packaging.

Descale cycle ("dESC"): takes about 1.5 hours. Machine must be off and cooled for at least 1 hour first. From off, hold 1 CUP then POWER for 3 seconds ("rSEt"), select "dESC", open the valves with a flathead screwdriver, dissolve 1 sachet of descaling powder/solution in 1L water and run it through, drain, then flush twice.

Water filter: discard the old one, wash the holder, insert a new filter, fill with cold water. Grinder: conical burrs - unlock, empty, run empty, clean; remove the tamping fan with a magnet. Steam wand: wipe and purge; soak with cleaning powder (1 packet in 240ml hot water, 20 minutes) if still blocked.

SES985 the Oracle™ Jet

Cleaning cycle: on-screen/Settings guided, back-flush style (the display specifies where to add the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution and how much to use). Also run a clear-water backflush after each session (disc fitted, 20-second brew).

Descale cycle: Settings → Descale, fully on-screen guided (the display specifies the water tank fill amount) - recommended even with the water filter fitted if scale is visibly present.

Water filter: every 3 months. At water hardness 4-5, switch water source instead of relying on the filter alone. Steam wand: wipe every session; Settings-guided cleaning; make sure the tip is refastened tightly. Filter basket: soak in cleaning powder for about 20 minutes. Grinder/conical burrs: the cleaning procedure differs before vs. after production code 2538 - check which applies. Tamper height is not user-adjustable on this model - contact Sage Customer Service if it needs attention.

Beans Never use unroasted or under-roasted beans on this model.
Known issue - stuck latch The latch can get stuck due to scale buildup. Fix: mix descaler with a little water into a paste, apply to the edge of the latch, and leave a few minutes. Do not force the latch open - risk of damage. If it's still stuck after the descaler treatment, proceed with a repair. See also the Oracle Jet tamper fan policy for the other known Oracle-family issue.
SES990 the Oracle™ Touch

Cleaning cycle: roughly every 200 shots, or Settings → Clean Cycle (back-flush style, on-screen guided for where to add the cleaning disc and descaling powder/solution and how much to use). Also run a clear-water backflush each session (disc fitted, Brew button, 20 seconds × 5).

Descale cycle: fully on-screen guided (the display specifies the water tank fill amount), takes about 1.5 hours. Machine must be off and cooled for at least 1 hour first. Never attempt it outside the guided menu.

Water filter: every 3 months; at hardness 4-5, switch water source. Grinder/conical burrs: 15-step clean - remove the hopper, run empty, brush, and remove the tamping fan with a magnet. Steam wand: wipe/purge every use; soak with cleaning powder (1 packet in 240ml hot water, 20 minutes) if blocked, and refasten the tip tightly. Filter basket: soak in dissolved descaling powder/solution for about 20 minutes.

Transport / storage Drain both boilers fully before transporting or storing the machine, as an anti-freeze precaution.

Talking the Customer Through It

What's confirmed vs. suggested, in this section The steps, button combinations, and cadence above are drawn directly from each model's own documentation. The example phrasing below is suggested wording in the ANCHOR house tone - it is not a verbatim script from the source documents. Use your own words; don't present it as an official quote.
Two ways to run the same conversation, depending on the customer
Moment in the callKnowledgeable customerCustomer who's unsure / new to the machine
Working out which cycle they need Ask directly and use the same terms they will: "Are you seeing a CLEAN ME or a descale alert, or is this routine maintenance?" Confirm the model, then go straight to that model's card. Explain the difference in plain terms before asking anything technical: "There are two things your machine asks for - a quick clean for coffee residue, and a descale for mineral buildup from the water. Which light or message are you seeing?"
Giving the steps Read the button sequence and cadence straight from the model card - they can follow the exact combination unaided. Break it into one instruction at a time and confirm before moving on: "Let's do this together, one step at a time - tell me when you're ready for the next one." Say "press and hold the 1 cup button and the power button together" rather than "1+2 CUP+POWER."
Setting expectations Mention timing and any hard rules up front (e.g. "cooled for at least an hour first" on the Dual Boiler/Oracle, "never remove the tank mid-cycle" on the Touch models) - they'll want to plan around it. Reassure first, then explain: "This is completely normal maintenance, not a sign something's broken. It takes about [time] - I'll stay on with you if that helps."
Applies to every customer, either way Confirm the exact model before giving any button sequence - they differ enough between models that guessing risks sending a customer into the wrong menu. If in doubt, point them to Model Specifications to confirm the SKU first.

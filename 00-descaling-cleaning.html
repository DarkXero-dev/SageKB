Every model needs a cleaning cycle and a descale cycle on its own schedule. This page covers why it matters, the difference between the two, the general care rules that apply to every machine, the exact steps for all 11 models as documented, and how to walk a customer through it - whether they're comfortable following technical instructions or need it broken down step by step.

Why It Matters

Straight from the source "Machine hygiene: adherence to cleaning/descaling cycle is a MUST, in addition to daily cleaning." The Perfect Single Shot Basics list is just as direct: "descaling/cleaning cadence is a MUST" alongside pressure, dose, and tamp.

Almost every "no steam," "no hot water," "espresso only drips or not at all," and "steam wand blocked" row across the Common Issues table and every per-model fault table lists needs descaling or needs cleaning as a possible cause. A customer who's fallen behind on both is the single most common reason a machine "suddenly" stops working right.

Cleaning vs. Descaling

Cleaning Cycle

Fits the cleaning disc into the basket with descaling powder/solution on top, then runs a cycle that intermittently flushes water through to dissolve it and flush it back into the group head - confirmed in the SES875 and SES876 official user guides. Grinder-equipped models also get a separate grinder cleaning cycle - documented as clearing oil build-up (Barista Touch) and blockages between the burrs and outlet.

Triggered by an extraction counter (200 shots on several models) or a CLEAN ME / Clean Cycle alert.

Descale Cycle

Clears scale (limescale) buildup that forms in the boiler, water lines, and steam wand from the water passing through the machine. Uses a descaling powder or sachet dissolved in water; the water filter is removed first on most models.

Triggered on a schedule tied to water hardness - the source material sets a faster cadence for harder water (e.g. every 60 days at hardness 6 vs. every 90 days at hardness 4 on the Bambino/Barista Express), or by a DESCALE alert.

General Care Rules (All Models)

Non-negotiables - every model, every time
  • Never immerse the cord, plug, or machine body in water.
  • Hand-wash removable parts only - nothing on any of these machines is dishwasher safe.
  • Never use abrasive cleaners, abrasive pads, or alkaline cleaning agents.
  • Avoid demineralised, distilled, or heavily-filtered (RO) water - use cold filtered tap water.
  • Replace the water filter every 2-3 months (exact interval is model-dependent - see the model card below).
  • Use freshly roasted whole beans; never grind unroasted or under-roasted beans.
  • If a fault won't clear after the recommended cool-down/retry, escalate to the relevant Sage/Breville Customer Service Centre - internal repair attempts void the warranty.

Step-by-Step, By Model

Full numbered procedures below are transcribed from each model's official Sage user guide (the PDF instruction books, cross-checked against the internal training material) - button names, menu paths, timings, and quantities included, not summarized. Button combos, screen paths, and cadence differ by model - always confirm the exact model before giving steps.

Where an exact quantity is documented SES875, SES876, SES920, and SES980 all specify 1 sachet of descaling powder/solution dissolved in 1 litre of water. SES500 and SES878 say to fill the tank to a marked DESCALE line and dissolve descaling powder/solution into it, without a separate sachet count. The touch-screen models (SES880, SES882, SES985, SES990) are fully on-screen guided - the display specifies the amount at the time. Where none of that applies, use the dose printed on the packaging rather than guessing a number.
SES500 Bambino™ Plus

Cleaning cycle - auto-detected at 200 extractions (1 CUP/2 CUP lights alternate-flash), or enter manually anytime by holding 2 CUP + STEAM for 5 seconds while the machine is off:

  1. Insert the cleaning disc into the filter basket in the portafilter.
  2. Place descaling powder/solution on top of the cleaning disc.
  3. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
  4. Empty the drip tray and replace it on the machine. Place a 2L container under the portafilter and steam wand, sitting on the drip tray.
  5. Fill the water tank to the MAX line and reattach to the machine.
  6. Press and hold 1 CUP + 2 CUP for 5 seconds to enter cleaning cycle mode.
  7. The 1 CUP and 2 CUP buttons stay lit - press either one to begin. The lights alternate-flash while the cycle runs.
  8. Once complete, the machine returns to ready mode.
  9. Remove and rinse the portafilter, cleaning disc, drip tray, and container.

Descale cycle - the machine indicates it needs descaling when 1 CUP + STEAM and the 2 CUP button flash alternately for 15 seconds (pressing any of these buttons cancels the alert). Cadence: every 90 days at water hardness 4, every 60 days at hardness 6:

  1. Empty the drip tray and reinsert it.
  2. Fill the water tank to the DESCALE line with hot tap water, then add and dissolve descaling powder/solution by stirring gently.
  3. Place a 2L container under the group head and steam wand.
  4. Turn the machine off (press 1 CUP + STEAM together) and let it cool completely.
  5. With the machine off, press and hold 1 CUP + STEAM for 5 seconds to enter descale mode.
  6. The 1 CUP and STEAM buttons illuminate when the machine is ready to start.
  7. Press 1 CUP or STEAM to begin - these buttons alternate-flash throughout the cycle.
  8. Once the descale solution is used up, the cycle pauses. Refill the tank to MAX and empty the waste water container, then replace both.
  9. Press 1 CUP or STEAM to resume.
  10. When descaling is complete, 1 CUP + STEAM alternate-flash, then the machine goes into standby mode.
  11. Remove and empty the waste water container. Rinse the drip tray and water tank and dry thoroughly before replacing them.
Warning Do not immerse the power cord, plug, or appliance in water or any other liquid. The water tank should never be removed or completely emptied during descaling. Uses a 2L descale container.

Steam wand: wipe and purge after every use. Water filter: replace every 3 months.

SES870 Barista Express™ (Safety Guide edition)

Same physical machine as SES875 below - use that card for the full cleaning/descale sequence. This document is a pictorial safety guide only (no printed step text for cleaning/descaling); it only confirms: the CLEAN ME light means the machine needs cleaning or descaling, and the water filter is replaced approximately every 90 days.

SES875 Barista Express™

Cleaning cycle - the CLEAN ME light illuminates when required; this is separate from descaling:

  1. Insert the 1 CUP filter basket into the portafilter, followed by the supplied cleaning disc, followed by descaling powder/solution on top.
  2. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
  3. Ensure the water tank is filled with cold water and the drip tray is empty.
  4. Press the POWER button to switch the machine off.
  5. Press 1 CUP + 2 CUP simultaneously, then also press POWER. Hold all 3 buttons together for about 3 seconds.
  6. The cleaning cycle runs for about 5 minutes. Water intermittently flushes into the portafilter to dissolve it and flush back into the group head (this only works if the cleaning disc has a hole in the middle - a disc without a hole should let hardly any water through).
  7. Remove the portafilter and confirm it has fully dissolved. If not, repeat from step 4 until it has.
  8. Once fully dissolved, remove the cleaning disc, reinsert the portafilter, and press 2 CUP to rinse the brew head. Place a container under the portafilter spout to catch splashes, and wipe any off the machine afterward.

Descale cycle - note: this model does not automatically prompt a reminder to descale (unlike the cleaning-cycle reminder). Recommended every 90 days at hardness 4, every 60 days at hardness 6:

  1. Fully dissolve 1 sachet of descaling powder/solution into 1 litre of cold tap water, then fill the water tank (start the process only after the powder/solution is fully dissolved).
  2. Press and hold 2 CUP, then press and hold POWER simultaneously, to enter descale mode. POWER flashes continuously while the unit heats up. It's ready when POWER, 1 CUP, the CLEAN ME indicator, and the HOT WATER/STEAM indicator are all solid.
  3. Press 1 CUP to start descaling the coffee component - about 25 seconds. The 1 CUP button flashes continuously, then stays solid once finished. Press 1 CUP again to stop early if needed.
  4. Turn the steam dial LEFT toward the STEAM icon to descale the steam component (about 13 seconds).
  5. Turn the steam dial RIGHT toward the HOT WATER icon to descale that component (about 8 seconds).
  6. Repeat steps 3-5 to fully empty the tank - about 0.5L of solution should remain in the tank after the first pass.
  7. Remove the water tank, rinse thoroughly, then refill with fresh cold water to the 2L max line and repeat steps 4-6 to rinse out any remaining solution.
  8. Press POWER to quit the descaling process - the LED lights switch off.
Warning Do not immerse the power cord, plug, or appliance in water. The water tank should never be removed or completely emptied during descaling. Do not use bottled water - it may contain dissolved solids that leave deposits inside the machine. The machine exits descale mode automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity; resume from step 3.

Water filter: soak 5 minutes before fitting, then replace every 90 days or 40L (60L at hardness 2). Steam wand: wipe and purge after every use. Grinder: if blocked between the burrs or outlet, advanced cleaning needs a 10mm socket. Group head seal: if leaking, replace the silicone seal with an Allen key.

SES876 Barista Express™ Impress

Cleaning cycle - this is a back-flushing cycle, separate from descaling. The CLEAN/DESCALE light flashes when a clean is due:

  1. Insert the 1 CUP filter basket into the portafilter, followed by the supplied cleaning disc, followed by descaling powder/solution on top.
  2. Lock the portafilter into the group head and place a large container beneath the portafilter spouts.
  3. Ensure the water tank is filled with cold water and the drip tray is empty.
  4. Press the POWER button to switch the machine off.
  5. Press and hold 1 CUP, 2 CUP, and POWER together for 3 seconds.
  6. The cycle runs about 5 minutes - water intermittently flushes into the portafilter to dissolve it and flush back into the group head. No water should come through the portafilter itself.
  7. Remove the portafilter and confirm it's fully dissolved. If not, repeat from step 4 until it is.
  8. Once fully dissolved, remove the cleaning disc, reinsert the portafilter, and press 2 CUP to rinse the brew head. Place a container under the spout to catch splashes, and wipe any off the machine afterward.

Descale cycle - the CLEAN/DESCALE light shows solid when required:

  1. Fully dissolve 1 sachet of descaling powder/solution into 1 litre of water, then fill the water tank (start only after it's fully dissolved).
  2. Ensure the machine is off, then press and hold 2 CUP + POWER simultaneously for 5 seconds to enter descale mode.
  3. Press 2 CUP to start descaling the coffee component - about 25 seconds. The 2 CUP button flashes continuously, then stays solid once finished. Press 2 CUP again to stop early if needed.
  4. Turn the steam dial RIGHT toward the STEAM icon to descale the steam component (about 13 seconds).
  5. Turn the steam dial LEFT toward the HOT WATER icon to descale that component (about 8 seconds).
  6. Repeat steps 3-5 to complete the process - about 0.5L of solution should remain in the tank after the first pass.
  7. Remove the water tank, rinse thoroughly, refill with fresh cold water to the 2L max line, and repeat steps 4-6 to rinse out residue.
  8. Press POWER to quit the descaling process - the LED lights switch off.
Warning Ensure the water filter is removed from the tank before adding descaling powder/solution. Do not use bottled water when descaling. The machine exits descale mode automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity; resume from step 2.

Water filter: replace every 3 months (soak 5 minutes, rinse 5 seconds). Grinder: conical burrs need brush cleaning; advanced disassembly if blocked. Tamp system: brush clean. Steam wand: wipe, purge, and unblock as needed. Group head seal: periodic silicone seal replacement.

SES878 Barista Pro™

Cleaning cycle (FLUSH) - the LCD shows a FLUSH alert when required; separate from descaling:

  1. Insert the cleaning disc into the filter basket in the portafilter.
  2. Place descaling powder/solution on top of the cleaning disc.
  3. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
  4. Empty the drip tray and replace it on the machine.
  5. Fill the water tank to the MAX line, then reattach.
  6. Press MENU to enter the main menu.
  7. Use the GRIND AMOUNT dial to navigate to FLUSH, then press to select. The 1 CUP button illuminates.
  8. Press 1 CUP to start - remaining time is shown on the LCD.
  9. Once complete, the machine beeps and returns to READY mode. If any residue remains on the cleaning disc, repeat the cycle.
  10. Empty and rinse the portafilter, cleaning disc, and drip tray.

Descale cycle - the LCD shows a DESCALE alert when required:

  1. Empty the drip tray and reattach. Remove the water tank and remove the filter from it.
  2. Fill the water tank to the DESCALE line indicated, then add descaling powder/solution to the water. Reattach the tank.
  3. Press MENU, use the GRIND AMOUNT dial to navigate to DESCALE, then press to select. The 1 CUP button illuminates.
  4. Press 1 CUP to start. The cycle runs in 3 stages - coffee, hot water, steam - beeping after each. Turn the STEAM DIAL to the HOT WATER/STEAM position when the LCD indicates.
  5. Once the descale cycle finishes, the machine needs a rinse cycle - the 1 CUP button illuminates again. As before, turn the STEAM DIAL when indicated.
  6. Empty and replace the drip tray. Empty any remaining descaling liquid from the tank, fill to MAX, and reattach.
  7. Press 1 CUP to start the rinse cycle - also 3 stages, shown on the LCD, beeping after each.
  8. Once the rinse cycle completes, the machine beeps and returns to READY mode.
  9. Remove and rinse the drip tray and water tank. Dry thoroughly before replacing.
Warning Ensure the water filter is removed from the tank before adding descaling powder/solution.

Grinder: conical burrs - unlock, empty, run empty, then brush; advanced disassembly for a blockage (burrs are sharp, handle with care). Steam wand: wipe, purge, and unblock as needed. Group head: wipe the shower screen, and periodically purge with an empty basket.

SES880 Barista Touch™

Cleaning cycle: a message prompt appears when required. This is a back-flushing cycle, separate from descaling - follow the instructions on the touch screen.

Descale cycle: go to Settings and select Descale cycle, then follow the on-screen instructions. Recommended even with the water filter fitted if there's scale build-up visible in the tank.

Grinder cleaning cycle (removes oil build-up on the burrs) - a 7-step pictorial sequence:

  1. Unlock the hopper.
  2. Empty the hopper.
  3. Replace the hopper.
  4. Lock the hopper.
  5. Run the grinder until empty.
  6. Unlock and remove the hopper.
  7. Unlock the top burr, remove it, and clean both the top and lower burr with a brush; clean the chute with a brush.
Warning The grinder burrs are extremely sharp - take care during this cleaning step. Never fully remove or empty the water tank mid-descale.

Steam wand: Go to Settings → Steam wand cleaning and follow the on-screen guide. Water filter: every 90 days / 40L (60L at hardness 2).

SES882 Barista Touch™ Impress

Cleaning cycle: a message prompt appears when required. This is a back-flushing cycle, separate from descaling - follow the instructions on the touch screen.

Descale cycle: go to Settings and select Descale cycle, then follow the on-screen instructions. Recommended even with the water filter fitted if there's scale build-up visible in the tank.

Grinder cleaning cycle - a 14-step pictorial sequence:

  1. Unlock the hopper, empty it, replace it, then lock it.
  2. Run the grinder until empty, then unlock and remove the hopper.
  3. Unlock the top burr and remove it. Clean the top burr with a brush, then the lower burr with a brush.
  4. Remove the tamp cover, clean the chute with a brush, then replace the tamp cover.
Beans Never use unroasted or under-roasted beans - risk of burr damage and a safety issue on this model specifically. Never use bottled water when descaling, and never fully empty the tank mid-cycle.

Water filter: every 3 months (soak 5 minutes). Filter basket: if the holes become blocked, dissolve descaling powder/solution in hot water and soak the filter basket and portafilter for about 20 minutes, then rinse thoroughly. Steam wand: Settings-guided cleaning, or manual pin/soak. Group head seal: multi-step guided replacement in Settings.

SES920 the Dual Boiler

Cleaning cycle - the CLEAN ME! icon appears after roughly 200 shots:

  1. Press MENU until the CLEAN CYCLE icon flashes and PUSH is displayed.
  2. Insert the supplied cleaning disc, followed by descaling powder/solution, into the 1 CUP filter basket.
  3. Insert the portafilter and lock it into the group head.
  4. Ensure the water tank is filled with cold tap water and the drip tray is empty and inserted.
  5. Rinse the filter basket and portafilter thoroughly before use.
  6. Press the illuminated MANUAL button to start. The CLEAN CYCLE icon flashes and the machine counts down from 370 seconds.
  7. When finished, remove the portafilter and confirm it's fully dissolved. If not, repeat the steps above without adding more descaling powder/solution.

Descale cycle ("dESC") - allow about 1.5 hours total. If you've just used the machine, turn it off and let it cool for at least 1 hour before starting:

1 - Prepare for descale:

  1. Empty the drip tray and insert it into position.
  2. With the machine off, press and hold 1 CUP, then press and hold POWER, together for 3 seconds. "rEST" appears on the LCD.
  3. Press UP or DOWN to set the mode until "dESC" appears, then press MENU to enter descale mode.

2 - Empty boilers:

  1. Remove the grey silicone cover on the lower front panel marked DESCALE ACCESS. Place a cloth over the drip tray to stop steam escaping.
  2. Use a flat-head screwdriver to rotate the right screw counter-clockwise until the valve is fully open (steam may release). Once the water is completely empty, close the valve by rotating clockwise until seated - do not overtighten.
  3. Repeat the process on the left screw.
  4. Empty the drip tray (use caution - it may be hot).
  5. Empty the water tank and remove the water filter.
  6. Dissolve 1 sachet of descaling powder/solution into 1 litre of warm water and pour it into the water tank.

3 - Begin descale: press MANUAL to proceed. The machine fills the boilers with the descale solution and heats to temperature - water may flow through the group head. Wait for the countdown to reach 0 to let the solution dissolve scale buildup (or press 1 CUP to skip ahead):

  1. Place a cloth over the drip tray to stop steam escaping.
  2. Empty the boilers again (repeat steps 2 and 4 from "Empty boilers" above).
  3. Empty the drip tray and reinsert it.
  4. Empty the water tank, rinse it, and refill with clean water to the MAX line, then lock it back into position.

4 - Flush boilers: press MANUAL to proceed - a 20-minute countdown fills the boilers with fresh water and heats to temperature (or press 1 CUP to skip ahead):

  1. Place a cloth over the drip tray to stop steam escaping.
  2. Empty the boilers (repeat steps 2 and 4 from "Empty boilers" above).
  3. Empty the drip tray and reinsert it.
  4. Empty the water tank, rinse it, and refill with clean water to the MAX line, then lock it back into position.

5 - Repeat step 4 (Flush boilers) once more, to clear any residual descale solution from the boilers, then press POWER to exit descale mode - the process is complete.

Water Never use demineralised or distilled water in this machine.

Water filter: every 2 months. Steam wand: wipe and purge every use.

SES980 the Oracle™

Cleaning cycle - the CLEAN ME light illuminates when required, roughly every 200 extractions; separate from descaling:

  1. Insert the supplied grey silicone cleaning disc, followed by descaling powder/solution, into the filter basket.
  2. Lock the portafilter into the group head.
  3. Ensure the water tank is filled with cold water and the drip tray is empty.
  4. Press MENU and rotate the SELECT dial until CLEAN CYCLE and PUSH are displayed.
  5. Press the SELECT dial to start. The LCD shows a countdown timer in seconds.
  6. When finished, remove the portafilter and confirm it's fully dissolved. If not, repeat these steps without adding more descaling powder/solution.
  7. Rinse the filter basket and portafilter thoroughly before use, then extract an espresso to season the machine and purge any remaining cleaner from the system.

Grinder cleaning (a 15-step pictorial procedure):

  1. Unlock the hopper, remove the beans, then replace and lock the hopper.
  2. Run the grinder until empty, then unlock and remove the hopper.
  3. Remove the upper burr and clean it with the burr brush; clean the lower burr with the burr brush.
  4. Remove the tamping fan with the tamp removal magnet, then clean the grind outlet with the grind outlet brush.
  5. Replace the tamping fan by hand, push the upper burr firmly into position, and lock it.
  6. Insert and lock the hopper.

Descale cycle ("dESC") - allow about 1.5 hours total. If you've just used the machine, turn it off and let it cool for at least 1 hour before starting:

Prepare for descale:

  1. Empty the drip tray and insert it into position.
  2. With the machine off, press and hold 1 CUP, then press and hold POWER, together for 3 seconds. "rSEt" appears on the LCD.
  3. Rotate the SELECT dial until "dESC" appears, then press the dial to confirm.

Empty boilers:

  1. Remove the grey silicone cover on the lower front panel marked DESCALE ACCESS. Place a cloth over the drip tray to stop steam escaping.
  2. Use a flat-head screwdriver to rotate the right valve counter-clockwise slowly until fully open (steam may release). Once the water is completely empty, close by rotating clockwise until seated - do not overtighten.
  3. Repeat on the left valve.
  4. Empty the drip tray (use caution - it may be hot).
  5. Empty the water tank and remove the water filter.
  6. Dissolve 1 sachet of descaling powder/solution into 1 litre of warm water and pour it into the water tank.

Begin descale:

  1. Press 1 CUP - the LCD shows a 20-minute countdown while the boilers fill with descale solution and heat to temperature (water may flow through the group head). Let it reach 0 to fully dissolve scale, or press 1 CUP again to skip ahead.
  2. Place a cloth over the drip tray to stop steam escaping.
  3. Repeat the "empty boilers" steps above.
  4. Empty the drip tray and reinsert it.
  5. Empty the water tank, rinse it, refill with clean water to the MAX line, and lock it back into position.

Flush boilers: press 1 CUP - a 20-minute countdown fills the boilers with fresh water (or press 1 CUP again to skip ahead), then repeat the empty/rinse/refill sequence above. Repeat this flush once more to clear residual solution, then press POWER to exit descale mode.

Water filter: discard the old one, wash the holder, insert a new filter, fill with cold water. Steam wand: wipe and purge; if blocked, soak with descaling powder/solution (1 packet in 240ml hot water, 20 minutes).

SES985 the Oracle™ Jet

Cleaning cycle ("Clean group head" in Settings → Maintenance) - a message prompt appears when required, or start it manually via Clean Cycle in Settings. This is a back-flushing cycle that cleans the shower screen, separate from descaling, and requires an Espresso cleaning powder. Follow the on-screen instructions.

Descale cycle (Settings → Maintenance → Descale) - "Will require descaling powder or solution," per the machine's own on-screen description. Recommended even with the water filter fitted, if there's scale build-up visible in the tank. Fully on-screen guided.

Clear water backflush - recommended after each session, before turning the machine off: empty the drip tray, insert the cleaning disc into the filter basket, insert the portafilter into the group head, touch the brew icon and let pressure build for 20 seconds, then tap to stop and release pressure.

Beans Never use unroasted or under-roasted beans on this model - burr damage risk. Tamper height is not user-adjustable - contact Sage Customer Service if it needs attention.

Water filter: every 3 months. At water hardness 4-5, switch water source instead of relying on the filter alone. Filter basket: if the holes become blocked, dissolve descaling powder/solution in hot water and soak the filter basket and portafilter for about 20 minutes, then rinse thoroughly. Steam wand: clean after every use (wipe with a damp cloth); if blocked, Settings → Steam wand cleaning walks through a soak using steam wand cleaning powder (1 packet + 240ml hot water, 20 minutes, then purge and rinse); make sure the tip is refastened tightly afterward.

Known issue - stuck latch The latch can get stuck due to scale buildup. Fix: mix descaler with a little water into a paste, apply to the edge of the latch, and leave a few minutes. Do not force the latch open - risk of damage. If it's still stuck after the descaler treatment, proceed with a repair. See also the Oracle Jet tamper fan policy for the other known Oracle-family issue.
SES990 the Oracle™ Touch

Cleaning cycle: a message prompt appears after roughly 200 shots, or start it manually via Settings → Clean Cycle. This cleans the shower screen and back-flushes the group head - follow the on-screen instructions.

Descale cycle: triggered from the pop-up message, or via Settings → Descale Cycle - follow the on-screen instructions. Allow about 1.5 hours; if you've just used the machine, turn it off and let it cool for at least 1 hour first.

Warning Do NOT attempt to descale outside the guided Settings menu procedure - damage to the machine could occur.

Grinder cleaning (a 15-step pictorial procedure, same as the Oracle above): unlock the hopper, remove the beans, replace and lock the hopper, run the grinder until empty, unlock and remove the hopper, remove and clean the upper burr with the burr brush, clean the lower burr with the burr brush, remove the tamping fan with the tamp removal magnet, clean the grind outlet with the grind outlet brush, replace the tamping fan by hand, push the upper burr firmly into position and lock it, then insert and lock the hopper.

Water filter: every 3 months; at hardness 4-5, switch water source. Steam wand: wipe/purge every use; if blocked, soak with descaling powder/solution (1 packet in 240ml hot water, 20 minutes), then refasten the tip tightly.

Transport / storage Drain both boilers fully before transporting or storing the machine, as an anti-freeze precaution (same procedure as the Descale section's boiler-draining steps).

Talking the Customer Through It

What's confirmed vs. suggested, in this section The steps, button combinations, and cadence above are drawn directly from each model's own documentation. The example phrasing below is suggested wording in the ANCHOR house tone - it is not a verbatim script from the source documents. Use your own words; don't present it as an official quote.
Two ways to run the same conversation, depending on the customer
Moment in the callKnowledgeable customerCustomer who's unsure / new to the machine
Working out which cycle they need Ask directly and use the same terms they will: "Are you seeing a CLEAN ME or a descale alert, or is this routine maintenance?" Confirm the model, then go straight to that model's card. Explain the difference in plain terms before asking anything technical: "There are two things your machine asks for - a quick clean for coffee residue, and a descale for mineral buildup from the water. Which light or message are you seeing?"
Giving the steps Read the button sequence and cadence straight from the model card - they can follow the exact combination unaided. Break it into one instruction at a time and confirm before moving on: "Let's do this together, one step at a time - tell me when you're ready for the next one." Say "press and hold the 1 cup button and the power button together" rather than "1+2 CUP+POWER."
Setting expectations Mention timing and any hard rules up front (e.g. "cooled for at least an hour first" on the Dual Boiler/Oracle, "never remove the tank mid-cycle" on the Touch models) - they'll want to plan around it. Reassure first, then explain: "This is completely normal maintenance, not a sign something's broken. It takes about [time] - I'll stay on with you if that helps."
Applies to every customer, either way Confirm the exact model before giving any button sequence - they differ enough between models that guessing risks sending a customer into the wrong menu. If in doubt, point them to Model Specifications to confirm the SKU first.

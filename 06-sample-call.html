06 · Customer CareSample Call - Merged ANCHOR (Acknowledge+Honour)

A full worked transcript showing the ANCHOR model condensed to 5 beats by merging Acknowledge and Honour into one opening step. Scenario: Oracle Jet (SES985) overdosing, matching the known factory-defect escalation pattern from the Tamper Fan policy.

Merge rationale Acknowledge and Honour are both empathetic, pre-diagnosis moves - recognizing the issue's impact and the customer's trust/investment land naturally in the same breath, so they're delivered as one opening beat instead of two.

Agent: Albert · Customer: Mr. Whitfield · Machine: Oracle Jet (SES985), consistently over-dosing 21–23g despite correct grind setting, max tamp height, and standard double basket.

Transcript

Albert: Thank you for calling Sage Appliances Support, this is Albert. How can I help today?

Mr. Whitfield: Yeah, hi. My Oracle Jet has been over-dosing every single shot for about two weeks now. It's putting out 21 to 23 grams no matter what I do, and I've tried everything.

Albert: Have you contacted us about this before, or is this the first time?

Mr. Whitfield: First time calling, but I've been messing with it myself for a while.

Albert: Can you talk me through what has happened?

Mr. Whitfield: I dial it in the same way every time, grind setting hasn't changed, I'm using the standard double basket, tamp height's already at max, and it just keeps dumping way more coffee than it should. My shots are coming out bitter and I've wasted a full bag of beans trying to fix it.

A+H - Acknowledge + Honour

Albert: I completely understand how frustrating that is, especially when you've been carefully dialing it in and it still won't hold a consistent dose - that's not what you'd expect from an Oracle Jet. You've clearly invested in a premium machine expecting precision, and going through a full bag of beans trying to chase a fix isn't a fair ask. I want to make sure we get to the bottom of this properly.

Merge rationale: the first sentence acknowledges the technical frustration and impact on the customer's routine; the second, in the same breath, honours the customer's investment and effort - no separate beat needed before moving into diagnosis.

N - Navigate

Albert: Let me ask a few quick things so I can see the full picture before we go further. Can you confirm - are you still on the factory tamper fan setting, or has that been adjusted at any point?

Mr. Whitfield: No, I haven't touched anything on the tamper fan, it's still however it came from the factory.

Albert: Good, that's important - I want to flag now that I won't be asking you to adjust the tamper fan connector yourself. On a unit this new, changing that setting tends to cause more inconsistent brewing, not less, so we keep that one alone. What I'd like to try instead: can we do one targeted check together? I'll ask you to confirm your grind size dial reading, and then we'll try a couple of shots at one setting finer, just to rule out grind drift before I treat this as a hardware fault.

Mr. Whitfield: Okay, sure, I can do that.

(Two-minute troubleshooting exchange: grind size confirmed at 22, one setting finer tested, dose still reads 21–23g on repeat pulls.)

Albert: Thanks for trying that with me. Since the dose stayed high even after adjusting grind, and your tamper fan is still at factory setting with max tamp height and the correct double basket, this is lining up with a known pattern we've seen on some Oracle Jet units - a fault with the tamper-fan connector or double filter basket itself, not something caused by how you're using it.

C - Commit

Albert: Here's what I'm going to do. I'm going to log this as a suspected factory defect on the tamper-fan connector and double basket, and escalate it to our Task Force team who handle this exact pattern. I'll have an answer back to you within 2 business days on whether we're sending replacement parts or arranging a different resolution.

O - Own

Albert: I'm taking ownership of this from here - you won't need to explain the issue again if it gets passed along. I'm attaching the full details of what we just tested, your grind settings, and the tamper fan confirmation to the case, so whoever picks it up next has everything they need.

R - Reassure

Albert: We'll get this sorted properly, whether that's replacement parts or another option once Task Force reviews it - you won't be left chasing this yourself. Is there anything else I can help you with today, or a good number or email to reach you when I have an update?

Mr. Whitfield: Email's fine, you have it on file. Thanks for actually listening, that's more than I got trying to search this online.

Albert: Happy to help - you'll hear from us within 2 business days. Thanks for calling Sage Appliances Support.

Per-Model Detail

SES500 Bambino™ Plus - compact automatic, no built-in grinder

Rating: 220–240V~ 50–60Hz, 1300–1600W · Water tank: 1.9L removable · Portafilter: 54mm stainless steel · Hot water: 90°C (194°F) · Milk texturing target: 60–65°C · Tamp: 10–15 kg · Dose: 8–11g (1 cup), 16–19g (2 cup) · Cleaning/descale container: 2L / 68 fl.oz

Key features: 1 CUP/2 CUP/STEAM buttons with 3 milk temp levels (Warm/Ideal/Hot) and 3 milk foam levels (Low/Medium/High); automatic milk texturing with jug temperature sensor; auto purge steam wand; programmable shot volumes; manual pre-infusion; advanced temperature adjustment (±1–2°C); auto-off standby; auto-detects low water and cleaning/descale due (200-extraction counter); water filtration with 3-month reminder; extra-tall cup clearance.

Included accessories: stainless steel milk jug, 54mm tamper, steam wand tip tool, Razor™ trimming tool, Dual Wall + Single Wall baskets (1 & 2 cup), cleaning tablets, cleaning disc, water filter + holder, descaling powder.

SES870 Barista Express™ - Safety Guide edition

Same physical machine as SES875 below - this document is a pictorial safety/quick-start guide only, no formal troubleshooting table.

Rating: 220–240V~ 50–60Hz, 1560–1850W · Water tank: 2L removable · Bean hopper: 250g · Portafilter/tamper: 54mm, integrated removable tamper · Steam wand: 360° swivel

Key features: integrated conical burr grinder, GRIND AMOUNT dial, GRIND SIZE selector, programmable 1 CUP/2 CUP shot volumes, CLEAN ME light, espresso extraction pressure gauge, cup warming tray, BPA-free parts.

Included accessories: milk jug, Dual Wall + Single Wall baskets, water filter + holder, cleaning disc, cleaning tablets, hex key, cleaning brush, Razor™ tool, steam wand/basket cleaning tool, Dosing Funnel™ 54, descaling powder, drip tray guard (colour models).

SES875 Barista Express™

Rating: 220–240V~50Hz, 1560–1850W, 15-bar Italian pump · Water tank: 2L · Bean hopper: 250g · Portafilter: 54mm stainless steel · Grind: adjustable dial, e.g. setting 8 as starting point; extended range 1 (finest)–16 (coarsest) with burr adjustment

Key features: integrated conical burr grinder with adjustable upper burr, GRIND SIZE + GRIND AMOUNT dials, FILTER SIZE (single/double) button, hands-free grinding cradle, automatic and manual dosing modes, 54mm integrated tamper, programmable 1 CUP/2 CUP shot buttons (~30ml/~60ml default), Advanced Temperature Mode (±1–2°C), Auto Off after 30 min, 360° steam wand with Temp Control™ milk jug, espresso pressure gauge, CLEAN ME light, cup warming tray, water filter, auto purge, safety thermal cut-out.

Included accessories: Temp Control™ milk jug, cleaning disc, cleaning tablets, Dual Wall + Single Wall baskets, water filter + holder, cleaning brush, Razor™ tool, cleaning tool (pin + spanner), Allen key, descaling powder, Dosing Funnel™ 54.

SES876 Barista Express™ Impress

Rating: 220–240V ~50–60Hz, 1560–1850W · Water tank: 2L removable · Bean hopper: 250g, locking · Portafilter: 54mm stainless steel · Grind: 25 settings (starting point No.16) · Dose: 8–11g (1 cup), 16–19g (2 cup) · Tamp force: precise 10kg impression, 7° "barista twist" finish · Hot water: up to 240ml per dispense, ~90°C · Milk texturing target: 60–65°C · Water temp adjustment: ±2°C in 1°C increments · Extraction guide: infusion 8–12 sec, balanced shot 25–30 sec

Key features: Thermocoil Precision Heating System, PID electronic temperature control, precision conical burr grinder with "Less Mess" fixed-position portafilter, dedicated hot water spout, The Impress™ Puck System (Intelligent Dosing with Central Grinds Loading, Assisted Tamping with Barista Twist, Precision Measurement Auto Correction), automatic/manual dosing with Manual Dose Adjust Dial, Dose Level Indicator bar graph, "A Bit More" correction, Dose Top-up, grinder purge function, espresso pressure gauge, manual pre-infusion, adjustable conical burrs, Maintenance Light (flash = clean, solid = descale), cup warming tray, water filter with 3-month reminder.

Included accessories: 54mm portafilter, milk jug, water filter + holder, Single + Dual Wall baskets, cleaning tablets, Razor™ tool, Allen key, steam wand tip tool, cleaning disc, descaling powder, cleaning brush.

SES878 Barista Pro™

Rating: 220–240V~ 50–60Hz, 1380–1650W · Water tank: 2L removable with replaceable filter · Bean hopper: 250g, locking · Portafilter: 54mm stainless steel, commercial-style spouts · Tamper: integrated, removable, 54mm · Grind: 30 settings, default 15 · Default grind durations: 9.5 sec (1 cup), 13 sec (2 cup) · Dose: 8–11g (1 cup), 16–19g (2 cup) · Tamp guide: 10–15 kg · Milk texturing target: 60–65°C · Auto-off: 30 min · Hot water outlet: auto-stops after 60 sec · Extraction guide: flow starts 8–12 sec

Key features: ThermoJet™ Heating System (extraction temperature in 3 seconds), integrated conical burr grinder (removable upper/lower burrs), adjustable grind size and amount, automatic dosing, hands-free grinding, Razor™ trimming tool, adjustable shot temperature, PID temperature control, instant hot water outlet, low pressure pre-infusion, volumetric control (preset/manual/programmable), Dry Puck Feature, large drip tray, adjustable conical burrs (extended range), LCD display with grinding/extraction animations, Reset to Defaults, First Use priming cycle.

Included accessories: milk jug, Dual + Single Wall baskets, water filter + holder, cleaning disc, cleaning tablets, cleaning brush, Razor™ tool, steam wand/basket cleaning tool, descaling powder, Dosing Funnel™ 54.

SES880 Barista Touch™

Rating: 220–240V~ 50–60Hz, 1380–1650W · Water tank: 2L removable · Bean hopper: 250g · Portafilter/tamper: 54mm · Steam/hot water limit: ~90 sec continuous, then auto-disables · Auto Off: 30 min

Key features: full-colour touch screen with swipe drink menu, PID-class touch interface, integrated conical burr grinder with hands-free grinding cradle, automatic/manual dosing, preset drinks (Cappuccino, Espresso single/double/custom, Long Black S/M/L, Americano S/M/L, Café Crema S/M/L, Hot Water), "Add New" custom drink saving, automatic milk texturing (auto shut-off) with milk temperature sensor and manual mode, Settings menu (descale, steam wand cleaning, factory reset, brew temp, guide/setup), Empty Me! drip tray indicator, auto purge, water filter with monthly reminder, extra-tall cup clearance, cup warming tray, wet & dry coffee separator drip tray.

Included accessories: milk jug, cleaning disc, cleaning tablets, Dual + Single Wall baskets, water filter + holder, cleaning brush, Razor™ tool, steam wand cleaning tool, water hardness test strip, descaling powder, Dosing Funnel™ 54, drip tray guard (colour models).

SES882 Barista Touch™ Impress

Rating: 220–240V~ 50–60Hz, 1430–1700W · Water tank: 2L removable · Bean hopper: 340g · Portafilter: 54mm stainless steel · Grinder: 40mm precision conical burr, adjustable (1 finest–25 coarsest) · Dose: 8–11g (1 cup), 16–19g (2 cup)

Key features: touch screen with barista guidance, rapid heat-up (extraction temp in 3 sec), PID temperature control, adjustable grind size/amount, Intelligent Brew Setup assist, Assisted Tamping (7° "Barista Twist," ~10kg), Razor™ trimming tool, automatic hands-free milk texturing with milk-type presets (Dairy/Almond/Oat/Soy), manual texturing mode, dedicated hot water spout (~90 sec limit), manual grind purge, manual override extraction, café size presets (Single ~30ml, Double ~60ml, Double+ ~90ml, Custom), custom drink saving (up to 15 characters), water filter system, extensive Settings menu.

Included accessories: milk jug, Single + Dual Wall baskets (1 & 2 cup), 54mm portafilter, water filter + holder, cleaning disc, cleaning brush, Razor™ tool, steam wand tip tool, water hardness strip, drip tray guard (colour models).

SES920 the Dual Boiler

Water tank: 2.5L, top-fill, removable · Portafilter/group head: 58mm, actively heated group head with embedded element · Tamper: integrated, removable, 58mm · Extraction pressure: 9 bar · Boiler: dual boiler system · Shot temperature range: 86–96°C (default 93°C, optimal 90–95°C) · Steam temperature default: 135°C (adjustable) · Steam wand: 360° swivel, 3-hole tip · Warming tray: convection-heated, 6-cup capacity · Pre-infusion: default Pr07 duration (recommended 5–15 sec), default PP60 power (recommended 50–65) · Default shot durations: 30/30 sec (1 & 2 cup) · Water hardness scale: 1–5 (default 3) · Milk texturing target: 60–65°C · Tamp guide: 15–20 kg

Wattage, voltage, machine dimensions/weight: not found in manual text.

Key features: backlit LCD (Shot Temp/Shot Clock/Clock display modes), espresso pressure gauge, manual + programmable 1/2 CUP volume control, Press & Hold bypass for full 9-bar pressure (group head purging), dedicated hot water outlet with dial, instant steam lever, dedicated descale access valves, integrated tool storage, drop-down swivel foot, Auto Start (settable wake time), Advanced Menu (reset, temp unit, audio, volumetric control choice of duration vs. volume), water filter with hardness test strip.

Included accessories: stainless steel frothing jug, Single + Dual Wall baskets, water filter + holder, cleaning disc, Allen key, cleaning tool (pin + spanner), espresso cleaning tablets, water hardness strip, Razor™ tool.

SES980 the Oracle™

Water tank: 2.5L, top-fill, removable · Group head/portafilter: 58mm, full stainless steel · Grind: 45 settings (1 finest–45 coarsest) · Default shot temperature: 93°C · Default steam boiler temperature: 130°C · Milk texturing range: 55–65°C (default 60°C) · Default shot durations: 20 sec (1 cup), 30 sec (2 cup) · Default pre-infusion: PP60 / PT07 sec · Auto Off options: 5, 10, or 20 min · Tamp force settings: 1–9, polishing duration 2–10 sec · Water hardness settings: Hrd1 (softest)–Hrd5 (hardest) · Boiler: dual boiler (implied, not explicitly named)

Included accessories: milk jug, water filter + holder, 1 & 2 cup baskets, cleaning disc, mini grinds bin, burr brush, grind outlet brush + tamp removal magnet, steam wand tip tool, Allen key, steam wand cleaning powder, espresso cleaning tablets, water hardness strip.

SES985 the Oracle™ Jet

Rating: 220–240V~ 50–60Hz, 1400–1600W · Bean hopper: 340g · Group head: 58mm, heated ("ThermoJet Heated Group Head"), adjustable ±1°C · Portafilter: 58mm stainless steel · Grinder: 40mm precision conical burr, auto-doses 22g automatically · Auto MilQ™ milk range: 45–75°C, 8 texture levels · Display: 5-inch HD touch screen · Connectivity: Bluetooth Classic + BLE, 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, 5GHz Wi-Fi (indoor-only in the 5150–5350MHz band)

Key features: ThermoJet Heating System (ready in seconds), Auto Grind/Dose/Tamp (22g auto), Auto MilQ™ automatic microfoam texturing (dairy/soy/almond/oat), Barista Guidance (detects over/under-extraction, recommends grind adjustment), Cold Brew and Cold Espresso functions, Auto Queue (sequences brew/texture steps), PID temperature control, dedicated hot water spout, Double Tap auto-purge, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth connectivity with app/cloud features, extensive Settings, adjustable conical burrs (procedure varies by production date code).
